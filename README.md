Problem Statement 1: Digital Contract Farming Platform

⸻

🚜 The Problem

Farmers frequently face uncertain market access, fluctuating crop prices, and the absence of guaranteed buyers for their produce. This uncertainty leads to:
	•	❌ Unstable and unpredictable farmer income
	•	❌ Heavy dependence on intermediaries and middlemen
	•	❌ Delayed, opaque, or unfair payments
	•	❌ Lack of transparency in contracts and settlements
	•	❌ Limited access to real-time market intelligence

These challenges significantly increase financial risk for farmers and reduce efficiency, trust, and transparency across the agricultural supply chain.

⸻

🎯 Objective

The objective is to build a Digital Contract Farming Platform that:
	•	Directly connects farmers and buyers
	•	Enables transparent digital contract agreements
	•	Supports fair price discovery and negotiation
	•	Ensures secure and timely payments
	•	Reduces dependency on intermediaries
	•	Provides farmers with a stable, reliable, and predictable market for their produce

This platform leverages AgriTech, AI, blockchain, and real-time market data to create a sustainable, farmer-first ecosystem.

⸻

💡 Solution Overview

Our platform is a comprehensive digital contract farming and price risk management system designed for farmers, traders, FPOs, and buyers.

It integrates:
	•	📄 Digital contract farming
	•	💹 Price hedging and futures linkage
	•	🤖 AI-powered price intelligence
	•	🔗 Blockchain-based contract verification
	•	💰 Secure digital payments

The solution ensures income stability for farmers, price transparency, and trust across the supply chain.
